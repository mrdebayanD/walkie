# Onboarding the family

You are the admin. Only you can add people, create channels, and change how long history is kept.

## First time (you)

1. Server running (docs/DEPLOY.md), iPhone app installed (docs/IOS_SETUP.md).
2. Open the **admin invite link** the install script printed, tap **Open in Walkie**, type your name, Join. You're in, as admin, in the **Family** channel.
3. Family tab › **My name and phone number**: add your number (shown to the family in SOS alerts so they can call you).

## Adding each person

1. Family tab › **Add people** › channel **Family** › "Who is it for?" (e.g. "Ma") › **Create invite**.
2. Send it the easiest way:
   - **In person**: show the QR code; they scan it with the phone camera.
   - **Text message**: type their number and tap Send.
   - **WhatsApp etc.**: Share invite link.
3. On their phone: install the app (TestFlight on iPhone, the APK on Android), open the link, **Open in Walkie**, type their name, Join. They see "Waiting for approval".
4. On your phone: Family tab › **Waiting for you** › **Approve**. They're in immediately.

Invites work once and expire after 24 hours. Delete unused ones from the Add People screen.

## Set up each phone properly (5 minutes in person)

- Allow **microphone** and **notifications** when asked.
- **Safety** tab once, so it asks for **location** (used only for SOS).
- Family tab › **My name and phone number** — add their number.
- Android only: follow the **battery guide** that appears (docs/ANDROID_SETUP.md).
- iPhone only: make sure **On duty** is on (Family tab › This phone).
- Try it: you hold the button and talk; they should hear you with their phone locked.

## Other channels

Family tab › **New channel** › name, colour, tick members › Create. To bring in someone new to that channel only, open the channel › **Invite someone new**. People in other channels don't receive Family SOS alerts.

## Teaching SOS

- It's on the **Safety** tab, never next to the talk button.
- **Hold 3 seconds** → a **5-second countdown** with a big **Cancel** → then everyone in Family gets a loud alert with location and a 15-second recording.
- If it was a mistake: **It was a false alarm**. When safe: **I'm safe**. Either clears it for everyone.
- In real danger, **call 112 first**. Walkie adds the family; it doesn't replace emergency services.
